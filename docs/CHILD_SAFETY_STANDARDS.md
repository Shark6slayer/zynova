# ZENOVA — Child Safety Standards

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

**Website:** [https://www.intellidev.in](https://www.intellidev.in)

**Standards URL:** [https://www.intellidev.in/child-safety](https://www.intellidev.in/child-safety)

---

## 1. Our Commitment

ZENOVA is committed to protecting children and preventing Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) across its services.

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child exploitation, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors and any content or behavior that endangers children.

---

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on ZENOVA:

- Creating, uploading, sharing or distributing CSAM.
- Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
- Grooming or attempting to groom children.
- Sexual solicitation of minors.
- Sexualized depictions of children.
- Offering, requesting or exchanging sexual images involving minors.
- Trafficking, coercion or exploitation of children.
- Encouraging or facilitating child abuse.
- Attempting to contact children for sexual purposes.
- Circumventing safety systems to exploit minors.

These restrictions apply to profiles, posts, comments, messages, videos, images, AI-generated content and all other platform features.

---

## 3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected child exploitation or unsafe content through available in-app reporting functionality.

Reports should include:
- The relevant account or content identifier.
- A description of the safety concern.
- Supporting information, where appropriate.

Users may also contact our designated safety team:

**Child Safety Email:** child-safety@intellidev.in

Reports involving suspected child exploitation will be prioritized for immediate review.

> **Important:** Do not download, copy, screenshot, or redistribute suspected CSAM when reporting it.

---

## 4. Investigation and Enforcement

Where we obtain actual knowledge of suspected CSAM or child exploitation, we will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and our published procedures.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

- Removing or restricting access to violating content.
- Suspending or terminating offending accounts.
- Restricting upload, streaming, or messaging privileges.
- Preserving relevant information where legally permitted or required.
- Escalating confirmed cases to appropriate authorities.
- Cooperating fully with lawful investigations.

---

## 5. Reporting to Authorities

ZENOVA maintains a process for reporting confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authority, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority, as applicable to the incident and legal obligations.

In India, reports will be escalated to the appropriate law enforcement authorities, Cyber Crime units, and relevant government reporting channels.

---

## 6. Child Safety Point of Contact

ZENOVA designates a responsible Child Safety Point of Contact to receive communications from Google Play, regulatory bodies, and other relevant parties concerning child sexual abuse and exploitation.

- **Designated Contact:** ZENOVA Child Safety & Trust Team
- **Email:** [child-safety@intellidev.in](mailto:child-safety@intellidev.in)

The designated representative is responsible for coordinating policy enforcement, reviewing incoming reports, liaising with regulatory bodies, and facilitating prompt safety action.

---

## 7. User Cooperation

Users are expected to report suspected child exploitation and cooperate with legitimate safety investigations.

False, malicious or abusive reporting may also result in account restrictions.

---

These standards may be updated as ZENOVA's services, safety systems and applicable legal requirements evolve.
